# César Blesa

Co-founder of [Volkra](https://volkra.es), where I build AI agents, process automation and data tools for SMEs. My background is in finance (Business Administration, Deloitte, Deutsche Bank) and I hold an MSc in Big Data Science & AI from the University of Navarra.

I like working next to the business: mapping how a company really works, connecting its systems and shipping something that runs in production.

## Projects

- **[volkra-reception](https://github.com/cesar379/volkra-reception)**: AI receptionist (voice, web chat and WhatsApp) that books, reschedules and cancels appointments in a real calendar. Multi-tenant SaaS with Postgres exclusion constraints, idempotent webhooks, row-level security and 117 tests. Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Claude.
- **[automatizaciones-ia](https://github.com/cesar379/automatizaciones-ia)**: AI automations for SME back-office. Includes a purchase review on the Holded ERP API that flags duplicate invoices, missing credit notes, price increases and wrong supplier records before month-end close and VAT filing. Python, 147 tests.
- **[Cesmar Advisor](https://advisor.cesmarsoluciones.com)**: crop nutrition advisory platform with verifiable AI. Answers are grounded in 211 technical data sheets and two product catalogues, and the server checks every quoted passage against the source before showing it. Private client area for plots, applications, lab analyses and photos, isolated with row-level security. React, TanStack Start, TypeScript, Supabase.
- **[PFS website](https://www.fertilizantesostenible.com)**: full redesign of a sustainable fertiliser company's site in Spanish, English and French. Technical catalogue of 55 products, blog, technical SEO (structured data, preserved URLs with redirects), lead capture forms and a private CRM. React, TanStack Start, TypeScript, Supabase.
